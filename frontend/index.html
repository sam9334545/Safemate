15-Minute Tuscan Garlic Basil Penne

A comforting, fragrant Italian pantry classic made with fresh basil leaves, blistered cherry tomatoes, thinly shaved golden garlic, and cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil.

PREP TIME 5 mins
COOK TIME 10 mins
SERVINGS 2-3 servings
DIFFICULTY Easy

Ingredients

  • 250g artisanal penne rigate
  • 4 cloves fresh garlic, sliced paper-thin
  • 1 cup sweet cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
  • Fresh genovese basil leaves (a generous handful)
  • Pinch of red chili pepper flakes
  • Freshly grated Parmigiano Reggiano & sea salt

Instructions

  1. Bring a generous pot of heavily salted water to a rolling boil. Cook penne until al dente (approx. 9 minutes).
  2. While the pasta boils, heat extra virgin olive oil in a wide skillet over medium-low heat. Add shaved garlic and chili flakes; gently infuse for 2 minutes until aromatic.
  3. Toss in the cherry tomatoes and cook until blistered and juicy.
  4. Transfer drained pasta directly into the skillet with 3 tbsp reserved pasta water. Fold in fresh torn basil.
  5. Garnish with Parmigiano Reggiano and cracked black pepper. Serve warm!
